Craft a recruiter-ready, ATS-friendly resume to secure interviews.

5 Golden Rules for Freshers

  • Stick to 1 Single Page: Recruiters spend ~6 seconds scanning resumes. Keep it concise.
  • Quantify Results: Use the formula: "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]".
  • Clear Section Order: Header & Contact → Education → Skills → Projects → Experience → Certifications.
  • ATS Standard Fonts: Use clean sans-serif fonts (Arial, Calibri, Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter).
  • Save as PDF: Always export and upload as PDF unless specified otherwise.

Impactful Action Verbs

Developed Engineered Optimized Automated Architected Streamlined Spearheaded Integrated Implemented

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Avoid unprofessional email addresses (e.g., coolcoder99@mail.com).
  • Do not add photo/headshots unless required in your country.
  • Avoid rating skills with percentage bars (e.g. "Java 90%").
  • Do not include physical home addresses or marital status.
